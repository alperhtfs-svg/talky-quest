Privacy Policy — Talky Quest

Last updated: [TARİH GİR] · Contact: [E-POSTA GİR]

Short version: Talky Quest is made for children aged 5–9. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics. The microphone is used only to check whether a spoken English word is correct, and audio is never saved.

1. Who this policy is for

Talky Quest ("the app") is an English-learning game intended for children. This policy explains, for parents and guardians, exactly what the app does and does not do with data. We designed the app to comply with the Google Play Families Policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including provisions relating to children.

2. Information we do NOT collect

The app does not collect, request, or transmit any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses
  • Dates of birth or age (beyond the app's general age rating)
  • Photographs, videos, or contacts
  • Location data of any kind
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or persistent user IDs
  • Usage analytics, crash telemetry, or behavioural profiling

There is no sign-up, no login, and no user account. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics SDKs.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

The core of the game is speaking English words out loud. To make this work, the app needs access to the device microphone.

WhatHow it is handled
When the microphone turns on Only while the child is actively answering a question. It is never left listening in the background.
What we do with the audio It is converted to text solely to check whether the spoken word matches the target English word.
Do we store recordings? No. The app never saves audio to the device and never uploads audio to any server operated by us.
Do we operate a server? No. We run no backend. The app has no account system and sends us nothing.
Important — please read. Speech recognition is performed by the speech-recognition service built into your device's operating system, not by us. Depending on your device, its settings, and whether an offline language pack is installed, that service may process the audio on the device itself or send it to the operating-system vendor (for example Google) for processing. That processing is governed by the vendor's own privacy policy, not ours. We do not receive, see, or retain that audio.

If you prefer that no audio leaves the device, you can install an offline English speech pack in your device settings, or simply play the game using the tap controls — every question in Talky Quest can be answered by tapping, with no microphone at all.

4. Playing without the microphone

Microphone access is optional. If you decline the permission, the game remains fully playable: every question can be answered by tapping the correct picture, number, or word. No feature is locked behind the microphone.

5. Data stored on the device

The app may store simple game progress (such as stars earned and prizes collected) locally on the device. This information never leaves the device, is not linked to any identity, and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

6. Children's privacy

Talky Quest is designed for children. Because we collect no personal information at all, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not create profiles of children, do not serve behavioural advertising, and do not sell or share data with data brokers.

Parents and guardians who have questions may contact us at the address below at any time.

7. Third parties

The app bundles no advertising networks, no analytics providers, and no social media SDKs. The only third-party processing that can occur is the operating system's own speech recognition service, as described in Section 3.
